What we did

01

Clip selection with intent

We stopped cutting highlights and started cutting hooks: a contrarian claim, a specific number, a story with a turn.

02

Scripting the open

The first line of every Short was rewritten and re-cut for someone who has never heard of James.

03

A consistent publishing system

A steady schedule, with every clip pointing back to the full episode and the channel.

04

Feedback loop

Each week’s numbers shaped the next episode’s clip plan.

What it led to

Shorts now reach 10,000+ views consistently, and the show is easier to pitch. Guests say yes more readily to a podcast they have already seen in their feed.
